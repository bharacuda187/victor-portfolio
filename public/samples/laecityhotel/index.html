Welcome to Lae City Hotel

Comfortable Stays. Exceptional Hospitality.

Lae City Hotel brings a fresh and comfortable accommodation experience to Lae, Papua New Guinea. Our elegant guestrooms, convenient facilities, and dedicated service are designed to make every stay enjoyable.

Conveniently located in the Top Town area of Lae and only minutes away from the main market area, the hotel provides an ideal base for business and leisure travellers.

Our Location

Located in the Heart of Lae

Lae City Hotel is located in the Top Town area of Lae, approximately 40 minutes from Nadzab Airport and close to the city's main market and commercial areas.

Lae is the capital of Morobe Province and Papua New Guinea's second-largest city. As an important industrial hub and port, it is an ideal base for business and travel throughout the region.
